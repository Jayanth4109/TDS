# Source photographs and transformations

`nixon.glb` reconstructs facial geometry from the Richard Nixon presidential portrait.
The photograph is a United States federal-government work, listed as public domain by
[Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Richard_Nixon_presidential_portrait.jpg).
Creator listed there: Department of Defense, Department of the Army, U.S. Army Audiovisual Center.
[Exact source image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/e/ec/Richard_Nixon_presidential_portrait.jpg).
Changes: facial landmark extraction, depth inference, texture mapping and an estimated rear head.

`morph.mp4` transforms the following photographs through alignment, geometric warping, blending
and video compression. The contact sheet is an extract of the same transformation.

* **Dario Fo:** Bernard Gotfryd, photographer; Bernard Gotfryd collection, Library of Congress.
  Listed as public domain / no known copyright restrictions on the
  [Commons file page](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Dario_Fo,_Italian_playwright.jpg).
  [Exact source image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/8/84/Dario_Fo%2C_Italian_playwright.jpg).
* **Frank-Walter Steinmeier:** Press Service of the President of the Republic of Azerbaijan;
  attribution **President.az**. Licensed under
  [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/),
  as recorded on the
  [Commons file page](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Frank-Walter_Steinmeier_in_2025_(cropped).jpg).
  [Exact source image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/4/49/Frank-Walter_Steinmeier_in_2025_%28cropped%29.jpg).
